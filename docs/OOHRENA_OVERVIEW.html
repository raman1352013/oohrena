OOHRENA

Ab Advertising Sirf Dikhegi Nahi… Track Bhi Hogi!

OOHRENA ek online marketplace hai outdoor aur digital advertising ke liye: hoardings, LED screens, bus aur auto branding, metro, mall screens. Brand yahan media dhoondhta hai, quote leta hai, booking karta hai, aur dekhta hai ki uska ad sach mein laga. Har kadam ka record ek hi ID se juda rehta hai.

LD Inquiry→ QT Quote→ BK Booking→ WO Work order→ CMP Proof→ PAY Payment
Kyun banaya

Aaj outdoor advertising mein kya dikkat hai

Aaj kal kaise hota hai

  • Client ko pata nahi chalta ki hoarding sach mein lagi ya nahi, aur kitne din lagi rahi.
  • Inquiry, rate aur photos WhatsApp ya phone pe ghoomte hain. Baad mein koi record nahi milta.
  • Beech mein kitne log hain aur kisne kitna rakha, ye saaf nahi hota.
  • Payment ka saboot alag, invoice alag, photo alag. Jhagda hone pe kuch dikhane ko nahi.

OOHRENA mein kaise hoga

  • Sirf OOHRENA team ki check ki hui media hi book hoti hai.
  • Har inquiry ko ek Lead ID milti hai, jo quote, booking, proof aur payment tak saath chalti hai.
  • Ad lagne ki photo upload time aur location ke saath aati hai, team verify karti hai, tab client ko dikhti hai.
  • Har payment ka saboot zaroori hai, aur doosra Finance user check karta hai.
Kaise kaam karta hai

Ek campaign shuru se ant tak

Ek example: ek brand ko Mumbai aur Gwalior mein festive season ke liye hoarding chahiye.

  1. Client

    Media dhoondhna

    Website pe shehar, area, landmark, PIN code ya map se hoarding dhoondhta hai. Har listing pe photo, size, rate ka andaza aur kaunsi dates khali hain, dikhta hai.

  2. Client

    Requirement bhejna

    Dates aur zaroorat likh ke "Request quotation" dabata hai. Turant ek Lead ID milti hai, jaise LD-2026-000001.

  3. OOHRENA team

    Check karna aur vendor chunna

    Har inquiry pehle OOHRENA team ke paas aati hai. Team sirf KYC-verified media owner ko kaam deti hai.

  4. OOHRENA team → Client

    Saaf quote

    Media ka rate, printing, installation, platform fee aur GST, sab alag-alag likha hua. Client approve karta hai, reject karta hai, ya badlav maangta hai.

  5. OOHRENA team → Media owner

    Booking aur work order

    Approve hote hi dates book ho jaati hain, aur koi doosra wahi dates book nahi kar sakta. Media owner ko kaam ka order milta hai.

  6. Media owner → OOHRENA team

    Ad lagne ka saboot

    Media owner lagane ke baad photo upload karta hai, aur campaign ke beech mein bhi check-photos daalta hai. Team verify karti hai, phir client ko dikhti hai.

  7. Finance

    Payment aur hisaab

    Invoice GST ke saath banta hai. Payment saboot (UTR ya receipt) ke saath record hota hai, aur ek doosra Finance user use verify karta hai. Media owner ka payout bhi track hota hai.

  8. Client

    Report

    Client ek click mein campaign report (PDF ya Excel) download karta hai: booking, photos aur kharcha, sab ek jagah.

Kisko kya milega

Brands aur agencies

  • Kai shehron ki media ek jagah, compare karne ki suvidha ke saath
  • Saaf, itemised quote, GST ke saath
  • Ad lagne ka photo saboot
  • Har campaign ki report download

Media owners (vendors)

  • Apni hoardings, screens aur vehicles list karke naye clients
  • Kaam ka saaf order, kab kya karna hai
  • Payout ki status ek jagah
  • Bank details encrypted rehti hain, sirf aakhri 4 ank dikhte hain

OOHRENA team

  • Saari inquiries ek inbox mein, koi lead chhoot nahi jaati
  • Sirf verified vendors ko kaam
  • Har action ka log: kisne, kab, kya badla
  • Revenue, conversion aur vendor performance ki reports

Growth partners

  • Naye advertisers refer karne ka seedha form
  • Apne leads ki status dekhna
  • Apni kamai (commission) track karna
  • Sirf apna data dikhta hai, kisi aur ka nahi

Hamare vaade

Client Transparency First

  • Demo ya bina check ki media kabhi "verified" nahi dikhegi, aur book nahi hogi.
  • Jo dates media owner ne confirm nahi ki, unpe saaf likha hoga "Subject to vendor confirmation".
  • Photo ke saath location waisi hi dikhegi jaisi phone se aayi. Hum "GPS verified" ka jhootha claim nahi karte.
  • Hum "Zero lead leakage" ka vaada nahi karte. Hum karte hain: centralised lead tracking aur poora audit trail.
  • Client aur vendor ka data surakshit: bank details aur KYC documents encrypted, sirf sahi role wale ko dikhte hain.

FAQ

Kya ye mobile pe chalega?

Haan, website phone pe poori chalti hai. Alag mobile app baad mein (Phase 2).

Online payment hoga?

Abhi payment bank transfer ya UPI se hota hai, aur saboot ke saath record hota hai. Payment gateway aur escrow Phase 2 mein.

Kaunsi media book kar sakte hain?

Hoardings, unipoles, wall wraps, auto aur e-rickshaw branding, bus aur cab, metro aur railway (jahan permission ho), LED screens, mobile LED vans, mall aur retail screens.

Vendor ka number client ko dikhta hai?

Nahi. Saari baat OOHRENA team ke through hoti hai, taaki har cheez ka record rahe.
